The case

CyberLeek: Everything We Know About The Group Dumping Alleged GTA 6 Gameplay

Clips since August 18. A manifesto. A meme coin. Take Two in court. We do not host a frame of the stolen footage.

Official Trailer 2 still. News commentary. We do not host leak clips.

A group calling itself CyberLeek has been dumping alleged GTA 6 gameplay since August 18. Rockstar has not confirmed a frame of it.

You have seen the clips in your feed. Jason shooting hoops. A map image. A wall with the word LEEK punched into it with bullets. That last one is why people think this is a playable build, not a stolen trailer folder.

We are a community site. This is the story everyone is arguing about. Here is the record, tagged as watch, not as gospel.

Who they say they are

CyberLeek is an unidentified person or group. Mashable and Polygon report it is not clear if this is one operator or several. They present as a consumer revolt. Watermarks on the clips say they are fighting for gamers rights.

They say they operate only a website and a Telegram channel. Accounts on X, Discord, and Instagram using the name should be treated as impersonators until proven otherwise.

There is no proven link to ShinyHunters. Jason Schreier at Bloomberg reported on August 21 that Rockstar still does not know who this is.

The clock

  • August 18: first videos and a claimed full map of Leonida hit the internet.
  • August 19: more footage. Watermark: NO PHYSICAL DISCS? THEN MORE LEEKS!
  • August 20: Jason flies a plane, then shoots LEEK into a wall. That clip is the playable build argument.
  • August 21: Bloomberg says Rockstar staff are furious and still have no ID. Token burn claims circulate.
  • August 22: website goes dark. Telegram shows copyright infringement. A seventh clip still leaked after the takedown.
  • August 23: we are writing this. Official Extended Look is still Thursday on Netflix.

What the clips claim to show

  • Jason playing basketball at a Keys style house
  • Driving, fighting, stealing a car, wanted stars
  • Taser use on NPCs
  • A claimed full map of Leonida
  • A biplane pass over Vice City
  • Shooting the word LEEK into a wall
  • Polygon reporting: the six star wanted system appears to be back

Insider Gaming and NateTheHate say the metadata looks old. Maybe 2024. Even if the clips are real, they may not match the November 19 build.

The manifesto

  • No digital preorders. Quote: Preorders were not created for gamers. They were created because physical discs had manufacturing limits.
  • No paid add ons that unlock single player content already in the game.
  • No killing single player when the servers die.

Stop Killing Games publicly rejected this approach. Using illegal means to make a point is unacceptable to us, they said.

The coin

CyberLeek launched a Solana meme coin, $CYBERLEEK. Holders vote with tokens on which clip drops next. Mashable on August 20 put the fully diluted value around $1.23 million, with top wallets holding over half the supply.

They offered paid ad slots. Price to open talks: about 400 XMR. You can hate digital only boxes and still clock this as a pump.

Take Two hits back

  • Copyright strikes are pulling copies of the videos down.
  • Subpoenas to Microsoft and Discord. Deadline reported as September 4.
  • Website offline the morning of August 22.
  • Strauss Zelnick said he is not worried the legal fight will dent GTA 6 sales.
  • Rockstar has issued no public authentication of the footage.

What we will not do

Vice City News will not embed, host, or restage CyberLeek clips. We will not help a coin. We will keep a running casefile until Thursday, when Rockstar speaks on Netflix at 3 p.m. Eastern.
