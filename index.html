NOV 6, 2025

The desk logs a second delay: launch pushed to November 19, 2026

Rockstar confirmed Grand Theft Auto VI would not make its previously stated window, pointing to the extra time needed to hit the level of polish the studio has built its name on. It's the second public shift on the calendar — the game had already moved once, from a vague "2025" target to May 26, 2026, before landing on its current date.

SEP 2022

The leak that started it all: 90 clips, one Slack breach, and a name — Jason and Lucia

Over ninety video clips of early development footage surfaced on a public forum, apparently pulled directly from Rockstar's internal systems. It was the first confirmation the public had that the next game existed at all — and the first time the names Jason and Lucia entered the record.

MAY 2025

A date, then a trailer — Rockstar breaks its silence

A release window finally went public, paired with a second trailer and a full overhaul of the game's official web presence. It didn't hold — but it was the first time "when" felt like a real question with a real answer, however temporary.

AUG 2026

"An Extended Look" — the desk is watching the clock

Rockstar has a longer look at the game scheduled for release this month. We'll be filing on it the moment it's public — not before.

THE PREMISE

Jason & Lucia

For the first time in the series' history, the game hands the wheel to two playable leads instead of one — and for the first time, one of them is a woman with a full voice performance, not a silent or secondary role. Jason and Lucia are the story's center of gravity, a pairing the press has repeatedly framed as this era's answer to Bonnie and Clyde: two people who trust each other because the world around them has given them no one else to trust.

The premise, as far as it's been made public: a score goes wrong, and the two of them end up on the wrong side of a criminal conspiracy that reaches across the entire state. It's a smaller, more personal setup than some of the series' past ensemble casts — which tracks with Rockstar's own framing of this as a tighter, more character-driven story than its predecessor.

THE MAP

Leonida, and the Vice City that isn't quite the one you remember

A modern Vice City

The reveal trailer placed the game in a present-day take on Vice City — the same neon-and-palm-tree template the series built its identity on in 2002, rebuilt from the ground up for a new console generation.

The Florida retcon

Longtime players will remember scattered references to a real "Florida" in past entries. Leonida's arrival quietly folds those references into the fiction — the state was always meant to be this one, not a real-world stand-in.

Scale — still unverified

Early leak-sourced claims described a map twice the size of Grand Theft Auto V's, spanning multiple cities and a high percentage of enterable interiors. None of that has been confirmed by Rockstar directly. We're flagging it as chatter, not fact, until it is.
